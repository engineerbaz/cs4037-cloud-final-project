# Project 7: Cloud Cost and Well-Architected Review

**AWS services:** AWS Pricing Calculator, Well-Architected Tool, Trusted Advisor (where available), Cost Explorer

## Client brief
The CFO asks why the monthly AWS bill doubled. You are the FinOps consultant.

## Requirements
Tick each box in your own `submission/REPORT.md` as you complete it, and attach evidence for every item.

- [ ] AWS Pricing Calculator estimates for three versions of one workload: all On-Demand, On-Demand with Reserved, and a mix using Spot
- [ ] A TCO comparison against running the same workload on-premises
- [ ] A review against all six Well-Architected pillars
- [ ] Ranked recommendations with estimated savings
- [ ] A before and after architecture diagram

## Defend It
Answer this in your report, and be ready to defend it live in the presentation. Every member may be asked.

> Which recommendation would you implement first, and which would you refuse even though it saves money, and why?

## Key learning
FinOps thinking, right-sizing, and balancing cost against reliability and security.

## Cost watch
Mostly a paper exercise with almost no spend. Share your Pricing Calculator estimate links in the report.
